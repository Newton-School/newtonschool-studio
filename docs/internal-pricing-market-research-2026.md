## Executive decision

NST Studio should not advertise itself simply as “50% cheaper than the market.” That framing makes price the product, attracts high-scope/low-budget buyers, and can imply half-quality. The stronger commercial position is:

> **Senior-governed product delivery at roughly 45–60% of the price of an established agency, achieved through a mentor-led delivery model—not through weaker engineering standards.**

The comparison must be made against the right peer group: established India-based product-engineering agencies and global boutique studios, not solo freelancers, no-code shops, or large consultancies. On that basis, the current package direction is broadly sound, but the ₹4L MVP needs a tightly constrained definition and the service catalogue needs more price bands.

## Scope and methodology

“All service-based companies” is too broad to be a useful market. This research covers companies that overlap NST Studio’s actual offering: custom software, startup MVPs, internal tools/ERP workflows, AI features and prototypes, backend/cloud work, applied technical research, and dedicated product teams.

The evidence combines:

1. Aggregated first-party client review data and provider listings from Clutch.
2. Regional outsourcing-rate research from Accelerance.
3. Public rate cards and fixed-price packages from software studios.
4. Published India-specific team and project bands.
5. Professional-services research on billing models and project margins.

Public price pages are marketing data, not audited financial statements. “Starting at” prices generally describe a narrow scope. Clutch bands are self-declared provider ranges, while its project-cost data comes from client reviews. Therefore, the recommendations use overlapping ranges rather than treating any single number as the market truth.

## Market bands by NST service

### 1. Discovery, architecture, and feasibility

**Observed market:** ₹1.5–7.6L for public fixed-price examples, with India-focused offers concentrated near ₹1.5–2.0L and global boutiques around $2.0k–8.0k.

**50% reference band:** roughly ₹0.75–2.0L when the comparison set is an India/global boutique mix.

**NST recommendation:**

- Discovery & Architecture Sprint: **₹1.2L** for one product, one primary workflow, and no live technical experiment.
- Complex Discovery: **₹2.0L** for multiple stakeholder groups, legacy integration, regulated data, or clickable prototype work.
- Credit no more than **50% of the discovery fee** against a build. Crediting 100% makes discovery look free and weakens the value of stopping after a valid “do not build” conclusion.

The current ₹1.2L entry price is defensible and already approximately half of a $2,400–3,500 global entry sprint.

### 2. Startup MVPs and new digital products

India-focused published estimates commonly place launchable MVPs around **₹4–20L**, depending on platform, integrations, and compliance. One 2026 India breakdown places directory/listing MVPs at ₹2–5L, marketplaces at ₹5–10L, B2B SaaS at ₹6–12L, mobile MVPs at ₹4–9L, and AI/ML MVPs at ₹8–18L. [Codingclave India MVP bands](https://codingclave.com/blog/mvp-development-cost-india-2026) Another guide places the broad India MVP range at ₹5–20L for 8–14 weeks. [Bright Infonet MVP guide](https://www.brightinfonet.com/insights/mvp-development-cost-timeline)

**50% reference band:** approximately ₹2–10L, but the bottom of that range is a prototype or very narrow single-flow product—not a broadly “production-ready MVP.”

**NST recommendation:**

| Tier | Price | Scope boundary | Typical team / duration |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Essential | ₹4.5–6.0L | Responsive web only; one core workflow; auth; simple admin; one integration; managed deployment | 2 builders + fractional mentor/QA; 4–6 weeks |
| Standard | ₹7.5–10.0L | 2–3 roles; richer admin; up to three integrations; analytics; stronger test baseline | 3 builders + mentor/QA/product; 6–10 weeks |
| Advanced | ₹12–18L | Multi-tenant, mobile, real-time, complex permissions, payments, migration, or regulated data | Custom cross-functional team; 10–16+ weeks |

The website’s current “from ₹4L” can only remain if it is explicitly a narrow Essential scope. Internally, sales should plan on a **₹6–9L average signed MVP**, not ₹4L.

### 3. Internal tools, dashboards, and ERP-like workflows

The market often labels this work “custom software,” “ERP consulting,” or “business-process automation.” Clutch places ERP consulting at $25–49/hour and most broader IT-services projects at $10k–49,999. [Clutch IT-services pricing](https://clutch.co/it-services/msp/pricing)

**NST recommendation:**

| Offer | Price | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Workflow Starter | ₹5.5–7.0L | One department, 2–3 roles, up to three workflows, dashboard, exports |
| Operations System | ₹8–12L | Several workflows, approvals, audit history, integrations, migration |
| ERP / Multi-department Phase 1 | ₹12–25L | Discovery mandatory; phase by module rather than one fixed mega-project |

Do not price a custom ERP as one fixed project before discovery. Sell discovery, then quote fixed milestones such as master data, workflow module, reporting, integrations, and rollout.

### 4. AI features, automation, and applied AI

Clutch reports a typical reviewed AI project band of $10k–49,999, an average reviewed project of $120,595, and a common provider rate of $25–49/hour. [Clutch AI pricing guide](https://clutch.co/developers/artificial-intelligence/pricing) India-specific public programmes show ₹2L for discovery, ₹4–6.8L for a proof, and ₹17.6L as the starting point for a six-week AI MVP.

**NST recommendation:**

| Stage | Price | Deliverable |
|---|---:|---|
| AI Feasibility | ₹1.5–2.5L | Use-case choice, data assessment, model benchmark, unit economics, go/no-go |
| AI Proof | ₹2.75–4.5L | Working technical proof on representative data with an evaluation report |
| AI Feature / Workflow | ₹4.5–8.5L | Production integration, review path, logging, fallbacks, evaluation suite |
| AI Product MVP | ₹8–15L | Product workflow plus AI layer, operational controls, deployment |

Always charge model/API usage, vector storage, labelling, and special hardware separately. Quote a capped experiment allowance, then pass through recurring usage at cost or cost plus a disclosed 10–15% administration margin. Do not bury volatile token or inference spend inside a fixed fee.

**NST recommendation:**

| Offer | Price | Typical content |
|---|---:|---|
| Architecture / Cloud Audit | ₹1.5–2.5L | Findings, target state, risk/cost plan |
| Backend or Integration Sprint | ₹3–6L | Defined API/integration outcome over 3–6 weeks |

### 6. Applied technology research

This work is hardest to estimate because the buyer is purchasing uncertainty reduction rather than a guaranteed build. Use gated stages:

| Stage | Price | Commercial promise |
|---|---:|---|
| Research Brief | ₹1.25–2.0L | Literature/market/technical review and experiment plan |
| Feasibility Experiment | ₹2.5–4.5L | One falsifiable hypothesis, prototype, measurements, recommendation |
| Working Research Prototype | ₹5–9L | Integrated prototype with reproducible results and handover |

Promise the quality and completeness of the investigation, not that the hypothesis will succeed.

### 7. Managed product teams / pods

Published 2026 India team planning ranges put a lean three-person pod at **$9k–14k/month** (₹8.6–13.3L), a balanced five-to-seven-person team at **$18k–35k/month** (₹17.1–33.3L), and larger teams at $40k–70k+. [NextPage India team guide](https://nextpageit.com/blog/dedicated-development-team-cost-india) Another public rate card lists small two-to-three-developer squads at $8k–15k/month and four-to-six-person teams at $18k–32k/month. [Algosoft squad tiers](https://www.algosoft.co/dedicated-development-team-india)

At exactly half of these market bands:

- A lean pod lands around **₹4.3–6.7L/month**.
- A balanced team lands around **₹8.6–16.7L/month**.

That makes the current ₹5L/month pod credible, provided it is a starter pod and its capacity is explicit.

| NST pod | Monthly price | Suggested composition | Commitment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Starter | ₹5.0–6.0L | 2 engineers, fractional senior mentor, shared QA/delivery | 3 months |
| Core | ₹7.5–9.0L | 3 engineers, fractional senior mentor, 0.5 QA, product/delivery oversight | 3 months |
| Scale | ₹11.5–14.0L | 5 engineers, named lead/mentor, QA, fractional DevOps/design/product | 6 months |

Sell a pod as **reserved capacity with a stable team**, not unlimited feature delivery. State holiday treatment, replacement policy, meeting load, time-zone overlap, support hours, and what happens to unused capacity.

## How companies charge—and when NST should use each model

Professional-services research still finds time-and-materials the most common billing method: a 2025 Harvest survey reported **57% T&M, 27% fixed fee, and 9% retainer**. [Harvest professional-services report](https://www.getharvest.com/hubfs/downloads/harvest-the-state-of-professional-services-2025.pdf)

| Model | How the buyer pays | Best use at NST | Main control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed scope / fixed fee | One price for named deliverables | Discovery, narrow MVP, audit, proof | Written assumptions, exclusions, acceptance criteria, change orders |
| Fixed milestones | Predetermined amount at each accepted stage | Larger MVP, internal tool, migration | Each milestone must have its own exit criteria |
| Time and materials with cap | Actual effort up to a budget ceiling | Legacy systems, uncertain integrations, rescue work | Weekly burn report and approval before cap increases |
| Dedicated pod | Monthly fee for reserved team capacity | Ongoing roadmap with changing priorities | Capacity, composition, notice period, and service level |
| Retainer / support | Monthly fee for defined response and small-work allowance | Maintenance after warranty | Included hours/capacity and response vs resolution SLA |
| Outcome / value based | Base fee plus payment tied to measurable result | Only when attribution and baseline are strong | Agreed metric, measurement window, floor and cap |
| Usage / pass-through | Buyer pays variable third-party consumption | AI, cloud, SMS, maps, data tools | Transparent metering and budget alerts |

Use a **hybrid base fee + variable usage + optional outcome bonus** for AI-heavy managed services. Do not promise pure outcome pricing where sales, operations, customer adoption, or the client’s data quality materially affect the result.

## The right way to apply the “50% of market” strategy

### Use a peer-market index, not one competitor

For every offer, record at least five comparable prices and calculate the median. Remove:

- Freelancer-only quotes with no delivery governance.
- Enterprise consultancies whose price mainly reflects procurement and brand overhead.
- “Starting at” offers that do not include a comparable scope.
- No-code prototypes when NST is offering production code.

Set NST’s target list price at **50–60% of the adjusted peer median**, then apply the cost floor below. If the cost floor is higher, the price must rise or the scope must shrink.

### Never let the discount break delivery economics

Use this internal formula for each package:

```text
fully_loaded_delivery_cost
  = people cost for planned effort
  + mentor/architecture time
  + QA and delivery management
  + project-specific tools and infrastructure
  + contingency (10–20% for fixed scope)

minimum_safe_price
  = fully_loaded_delivery_cost / (1 - target_gross_margin)

list_price
  = max(50–60% of peer median, minimum_safe_price)
```

Target a **minimum 40% project gross margin**, and preferably 45–50% on fixed-price work, before central sales/admin overhead. The 2025 Professional Services Maturity Benchmark reports project margins around 38.8% for software professional services and 35.6% for agencies, which is a useful warning line rather than an aspirational target. [SPI 2025 benchmark](https://get.kantata.com/rs/677-LEJ-696/images/2025-ps-maturity-benchmark.pdf)

Example: if a Standard MVP costs ₹4.5L to deliver including contingency, a 40% gross-margin floor makes the minimum safe price ₹7.5L. A “half-market” calculation that produces ₹6L is irrelevant—the project must be repriced or reduced.

### Keep an internal shadow rate

Even when clients see fixed prices, track planned and actual effort against internal shadow rates:

| Work type | Suggested internal sell-rate assumption |
|---|---:|
| Student / associate engineering | ₹1,250–1,750/hour |
| Experienced engineering / QA | ₹2,000–3,000/hour |
| Senior mentor / architect / specialist | ₹4,000–6,000/hour |
| Blended project planning rate | ₹2,000–2,750/hour |

These are planning assumptions, not a public rate card. Replace them with NST’s actual fully loaded costs once compensation, utilization, and mentor availability are known.

## Commercial terms to standardize

### Fixed-scope work

- Discovery: 100% upfront, or 70/30 for larger sprints.
- Projects under ₹8L: 40% kickoff / 40% accepted midpoint / 20% production handover.
- Projects above ₹8L: 30% kickoff / 30% design or architecture acceptance / 30% feature complete / 10% production handover.
- Client delay longer than five business days moves the schedule; longer than ten permits re-planning and a restart fee.
- Change requests are estimated and approved before work begins.
- Include a 30-day defect warranty for behaviour that contradicts written acceptance criteria; enhancements are paid work.

### Pods

- Bill monthly in advance.
- Three-month minimum for Starter/Core and six months for Scale.
- 30-day notice to resize or cancel after the minimum term.
- Define overlap hours and planned leave.
- Production on-call and weekend work are separate add-ons.
- Third-party costs are excluded.

### Discount policy

Do not negotiate the day rate downward. Trade price only for something valuable:

- 5% for full upfront project payment.
- 5–8% for a six-month pod commitment.
- Up to 10% for a 12-month commitment with quarterly prepayment.
- Reduce scope for any deeper price reduction.
- Never stack discounts beyond 10% without founder approval.

## Risks in leading with half-market pricing

1. **Wrong anchor:** prospects may compare NST with freelancers, making a fair price look expensive.
2. **Quality inference:** a numerical 50% claim can imply half the capability.
3. **Scope pressure:** low entry pricing encourages clients to treat exceptions as included.
4. **Mentor bottleneck:** the business model fails if senior review time is not costed and scheduled.
5. **Student utilization risk:** delivery cost rises quickly when rework, onboarding, or academic schedules are ignored.
6. **Case-study gap:** without proof, buyers will interpret the lower price as execution risk.
7. **Currency and payment risk:** export pricing needs an FX buffer and clear payment terms.

The safer narrative is “**agency-grade governance with a structurally lean delivery model**.” The commercial proof should be named mentors, review gates, acceptance tests, transparent scope, weekly demos, code ownership, warranty, and measurable case studies.

## Recommended rollout

### First 30 days

1. Cost the last 3–5 completed projects using actual hours by engineer, mentor, QA, and delivery role.
2. Establish fully loaded hourly costs and realistic billable utilization.
3. Define exact inclusions, exclusions, and acceptance criteria for Discovery, MVP Essential, and Starter Pod.
4. Use the recommended price bands in proposals, but keep only “from” prices in customer-facing material until ten deals provide conversion evidence.

### Deals 1–10

Track quoted price, won price, peer anchor, estimated hours, actual hours, gross margin, change requests, sales-cycle length, and reason won/lost. Do not optimize only for close rate.

### After 10 qualified proposals

- If win rate is above 45% and delivery margin above 45%, raise the band 10–15%.
- If win rate is below 20% but qualified prospects praise the scope, test packaging and proof before cutting price.
- If margin is below 35%, raise price or shrink scope immediately.
- Retire any package whose scope varies by more than 2× across buyers; it is not actually a package.

## Bottom line

NST can credibly enter at about half of an established agency’s price, but only by comparing like with like and enforcing scope discipline. The strongest launch portfolio is:

1. **₹1.2L Discovery** as the low-risk entry.
2. **₹4.5L Essential MVP** with a deliberately narrow definition.
3. **₹7.5L Standard MVP** as the expected core sale.
4. **₹2.75L AI Proof** as a separate pre-production offer.
5. **₹5L/month Starter Pod** with explicit capacity and a three-month commitment.

The current ₹9L Product Acceleration price is within the recommended band. The current ₹5L/month pod is also correctly positioned. The current ₹4L MVP should move to ₹4.5L or retain ₹4L only for a sharply bounded launch promotion—not as the normal price of a production-ready product.
